RIFTBOUND

A fractured world. One hidden saboteur. Collect rift shards, finish objectives, and survive the arena.

How to play
Move with the joystick or WASD. Pulse enemies, blink away, and scan glowing terminals. At 8 shards, activate the portal. Find the saboteur before they drain your energy.
